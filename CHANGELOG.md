# Changelog

## 0.3.0
- Keep recovery penalties per driver; preserve nitro and exclude recovered laps from records.
- Let rivals finish after the player, then classify adjusted times and show gaps/DNFs.
- Add three distinct handling profiles derived from the editable baseline tune.
- Share automatic gear/RPM/shift state between wheel torque, engine audio and HUD.
- Add passing-lane occupancy checks, rear-closing checks and stable overtake targets.
- Add a smoothed outside/apex/outside guide and car/weather-aware braking envelope.
- Add garage keyboard selection, a garage shortcut and window/fullscreen toggle.
- Expand regression checks for race fairness, finishing, gearbox, traffic and guidance.

## 0.2.0
- Replace transform-based driving with Rigidbody and four-wheel physics.
- Add physical AI, corner speed planning, traffic response and safe recovery.
- Add two circuits, three car choices, six-driver racing and time trial.
- Add garage, difficulty/weather selection, camera modes and detailed race HUD.
- Use Josefin Sans Regular, SemiBold and Bold with the original font license.
- Add layered engine/surface/boost audio, countdown/lap/finish cues and sound levels.
- Import Kenney CC0 car, racing and nature models with original licenses.
- Add track/race-rule and full physics validation, build tooling and repository checks.
- Separate runtime/editor assemblies and document design, research and contributions.

## 0.1.0
- Initial procedural Unity racing prototype.
