- Add physical AI, corner speed planning, traffic response and safe recovery.
- Add two circuits, three car choices, six-driver racing and time trial.

- Use Josefin Sans Regular, SemiBold and Bold with the original font license.
- Add layered engine/surface/boost audio, countdown/lap/finish cues and sound levels.
- Import Kenney CC0 car, racing and nature models with original licenses.
- Add track/race-rule and full physics validation, build tooling and repository checks.
- Separate runtime/editor assemblies and document design, research and contributions.
