## Audio

Engine combustion layers, tyre scrub, road/wind noise, nitro, collision and race
cues are original synthesized WAV assets generated by `scripts/generate_audio.py`.
They are included under the project's MIT license. No sampled recordings or
third-party sound effects are used.

License copies for the standalone player are retained in
`Assets/StreamingAssets/Licenses`; Unity bundles that folder in the native app.
